About Us

M.R.S. Enterprise was established in 2018 by passionate technicians hailing from the Antigua State College. During that time span in college, the founder and co-founder of M.R.S ENTERPRISE achieved a Pearson BTEC Level 3 Diploma in Construction and the Built Environment (Building Services Engineering) (QCF).

As we strive with a continuance to increase our levels of becoming UK based chartered Building Services Engineers, we have been proudly offering professional services to Antigua.
